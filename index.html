Safety First

Get Pattas Kadai - Safe Celebration Guide

Follow these essential safety precautions for a joyful & injury-free Diwali celebration with your family.

DO's

  • Always burst crackers in an open space clear of combustible materials and dry leaves.
  • Keep a bucket of water and sand nearby for emergency response and used sparkler dips.
  • Store crackers in a cool, dry place inside sealed cardboard or wooden containers.
  • Supervise children at all times while lighting sparklers, chakkars, and flower pots.
  • Wear snug cotton clothing and proper protective footwear while bursting fireworks.
  • Light aerial shells and rockets from a secure flat ground or sturdy launcher.

DON'Ts

  • Never attempt to reignite or inspect a cracker that failed to burst initially.
  • Never light crackers while holding them in your hands or inside glass/metal containers.
  • Do not light fireworks near electrical transformers, vehicles, or LPG gas cylinders.
  • Avoid wearing synthetic, nylon, silk, or loose flowing clothes near open flames.
  • Never throw lit crackers towards people, animals, buildings, or passing traffic.
  • Do not store crackers in pockets or close to hot kitchen areas.
